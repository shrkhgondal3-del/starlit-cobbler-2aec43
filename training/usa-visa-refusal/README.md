## Pathway

`U.S. Embassy refusal (Pakistan) → Reapplication / 221(g) → I-290B/AAO (if USCIS) → Narrow federal court (Mandamus/APA/PFR) → Settlement/mootness`

**Important:** Most F-1 / B-1/B-2 **consular** refusals under INA § 214(b) have **no AAO appeal** and are generally **not judicially reviewable**. The manual trains the team on the real remedies first.
