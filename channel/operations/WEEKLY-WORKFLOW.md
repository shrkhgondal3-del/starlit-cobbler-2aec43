# Saudi Gateway — Weekly Production Workflow

Repeat this cycle every week. Total time: ~12–16 hours/week (solo) or ~6–8 hours (with editor).

---

## Monday — Plan & Short #1

| Time | Task |
|------|------|
| 30 min | Review analytics: top video, worst CTR, traffic sources |
| 30 min | Confirm this week's 2 long-form topics from content calendar |
| 30 min | Update scripts if regulations changed (check MISA, REGA news) |
| 1 hr | Cut & publish **Short #1** from previous long-form |
| 30 min | Reply to all comments from past 7 days |

**Output:** Short published, week confirmed, comments cleared

---

## Tuesday — Record & Publish Long-Form #1

| Time | Task |
|------|------|
| 1 hr | Set up camera, mic, lights; teleprompter with script |
| 2 hr | Record A-roll (long-form #1) |
| 1 hr | Record B-roll pickups and pick-up lines |
| — | Hand off to editor OR block Wed for self-edit |

**If publishing today (batch producers):**
| 2 hr | Edit, captions, thumbnails |
| 30 min | Upload, SEO description, playlist, end screen, cards |
| 15 min | Pin comment, share on LinkedIn |

**Output:** Long-form #1 published (or raw footage ready)

---

## Wednesday — Edit & Short #2

| Time | Task |
|------|------|
| 3 hr | Edit long-form #1 (if not done Tue) |
| 1 hr | Cut & publish **Short #2** |
| 30 min | Write hub blog post summary for Tuesday video |

**Output:** Long-form #1 live, Short #2 live, blog post draft

---

## Thursday — Record Long-Form #2

| Time | Task |
|------|------|
| 1 hr | Prep script #2, lawyer quick-review if needed |
| 2 hr | Record A-roll (long-form #2) |
| 1 hr | B-roll and screen recordings (portals, diagrams) |

**Output:** Raw footage for Friday video

---

## Friday — Publish Long-Form #2

| Time | Task |
|------|------|
| 3 hr | Edit, captions (EN + AR), thumbnails (3 variants) |
| 30 min | Upload, full SEO package, playlist, end screens |
| 15 min | Pin comment, LinkedIn post, schedule Short #3 for Saturday |

**Output:** Long-form #2 published

---

## Saturday — Short #3 & Batch

| Time | Task |
|------|------|
| 1 hr | Cut & publish **Short #3** |
| 1 hr | Batch-schedule next week's Shorts (optional) |
| 30 min | Update content tracker with metrics |

**Output:** Short #3 live, tracker updated

---

## Sunday — Rest or Strategic (Optional)

- Research next 2 weeks' topics
- Outreach to lawyer/developer for guest segment
- Media kit updates
- Course/checklist product development

---

## Role Split (When You Scale)

| Role | Weekly hours | Responsibilities |
|------|-------------|------------------|
| **Presenter / Host** | 4–6 | Record A-roll, community engagement |
| **Researcher / Writer** | 4–6 | Scripts, regulation updates, SEO |
| **Editor** | 6–8 | Cut, captions, Shorts, thumbnails |
| **Growth / Ops** | 2–4 | Upload, analytics, partnerships, hub page |

**Hire order:** Editor first → Researcher second → Growth third

---

## Quality Gates (Never Skip)

Before every long-form publish:

- [ ] Lawyer reviewed regulation claims (or "verified on [date]" with source screenshots)
- [ ] Disclaimer in description
- [ ] Arabic subtitles uploaded
- [ ] 3 thumbnail variants ready
- [ ] Chapters added
- [ ] End screen configured
- [ ] Checklist/lead magnet link in description
- [ ] Added to correct playlist
- [ ] Pinned comment with engagement question

---

## Monthly Review (First Sunday of Month)

1. Export YouTube analytics CSV
2. Rank all videos by: views, CTR, AVD, subscribers gained
3. Double down on top-performing pillar (property vs. business)
4. Retire or re-thumbbottom 2 videos (CTR < 3% after 30 days)
5. Update content calendar with trending topics from comments
6. Refresh hub page with new checklist or lead magnet
