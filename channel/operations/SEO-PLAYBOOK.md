# Saudi Gateway — SEO Playbook

YouTube is a search engine. Every title, description, and tag should target a specific query foreigners type when researching KSA property or business setup.

---

## Title Rules

1. **Primary keyword in first 40 characters**
2. **Year stamp** for freshness: `(2026)` or `2026 Guide`
3. **Audience qualifier:** `for Foreigners`, `as a Foreigner`, `International Buyers`
4. **Length:** 50–65 characters ideal (truncation on mobile)
5. **Avoid clickbait** that regulations can't support — hurts retention and trust

### Title Templates

```
Can Foreigners [Action] in Saudi Arabia? (2026 Guide)
How to [Action] in Saudi Arabia as a Foreigner
[Topic] in Saudi Arabia: [Subtitle] (2026)
[N] [Things/Mistakes/Steps] [Topic] Saudi Arabia
Saudi [Program Name]: Full Guide for [Audience]
[City] Property [Year]: [Subtitle] for Foreign Buyers
```

## Thumbnail SEO (CTR Optimization)

YouTube can't "read" thumbnails for SEO, but CTR drives ranking.

| Metric | Target | Fix if low |
|--------|--------|------------|
| CTR | 6%+ | New thumbnail, sharper title |
| AVD (avg view duration) | 50%+ of video | Tighter edit, stronger hook |
| First 30 sec retention | 70%+ | Rewrite hook |

**A/B test protocol:**
1. Publish with Thumbnail A
2. After 48 hours, if CTR < 4%, swap to Thumbnail B via Test & Compare
3. Keep winner, document in content tracker

## External SEO (Hub Page + Google)

1. Each video gets a **blog post** on hub site (transcript summary + checklist) — doubles Google surface area
2. **Schema markup** on hub: `VideoObject`, `Organization`, `FAQPage`
3. Submit sitemap to Google Search Console
4. Build **backlinks:** guest posts on expat blogs, LinkedIn articles, Quora answers

---

## Algorithm Signals to Prioritize

1. **Session time** — end screens to next video (not random)
2. **Playlist binge** — order playlists as sequential courses
3. **Comments** — pin question; reply within 24 hours first 48 hours after publish
4. **Consistency** — same days/times (Tue/Fri 10:00 AM GMT suggested for UK/US overlap)
