## CHAPTER 1: What Is Premium Residency? (0:25–2:00)

Premium Residency is a long-term residency status granted to eligible foreigners outside the traditional kafala (sponsorship) system. Introduced as part of Vision 2030 to attract global talent and capital.

**Two main categories:**
- **Permanent (unlimited duration)** — for qualifying investors and high-net-worth individuals  
- **Annual (renewable)** — lower threshold, renewed yearly  

**Rights typically include:**
- Residency without a local sponsor  
- Ability to own residential and commercial property in approved areas  
- Conduct business within licensed scope  
- Exit and re-enter freely  
- Sponsor dependents (subject to rules)

## CHAPTER 3: Property Ownership Rights (4:30–7:00)

**[On camera]**

"This is why you're watching. Premium Residency holders can acquire **real estate in designated areas** — both residential and commercial.

**What you CAN typically do:**
- Purchase apartments, villas, land (in approved zones)  
- Buy commercial units for business use  
- Register ownership with REGA  
- Inherit and transfer (subject to regulations)  

**What you still CAN'T assume:**
- Ownership in every district of Riyadh or Jeddah automatically  
- Agricultural land (generally restricted for foreigners)  
- Property in holy cities (Makkah/Madinah — special rules apply)  

**The practical workflow:**
1. Obtain Premium Residency  
2. Identify REGA-approved property in an eligible zone  
3. Complete purchase and register title  
4. Maintain residency status (renew annual tier; permanent requires compliance with program rules)

## CHAPTER 5: Application Process (9:00–10:30)

**[On camera]**

"**Step-by-step:**

1. Visit the official Premium Residency portal  
2. Create applicant profile  
3. Select tier (permanent or annual)  
4. Upload documents: passport, financial proof, criminal record certificate, photographs  
5. Pay application fee online  
6. Await review and potential interview/request for additional documents  
7. Receive approval and residency card (iqama equivalent)  

**Timeline:** Weeks to a few months. Incomplete financial documentation is the #1 delay.

**Tip:** Use a licensed immigration consultant registered in KSA — not a random 'agent' on WhatsApp."

## CHAPTER 6: Is It Worth It? (10:30–12:00)

**[On camera]**

"**Worth it if:**
- You're buying property AND want long-term residency  
- You want business flexibility without a sponsor  
- You're comparing against Dubai Golden Visa and want a Saudi base  

**Maybe not if:**
- You only want one off-plan unit in a single giga-project (project-specific rules may suffice)  
- You're taking a 2-year employment contract (work visa may be enough temporarily)  
- You don't meet financial thresholds  

**ROI framing:** Don't calculate Premium Residency cost in isolation — compare total cost of residency + property + business setup against your alternative (Dubai, Bahrain, staying home and investing remotely)."
