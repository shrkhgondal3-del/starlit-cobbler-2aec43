## CHAPTER 2: Key Districts for Foreign Buyers (2:00–7:00)

**[Map on screen with district highlights]**

### Al Olaya & North Riyadh
- **Profile:** Established premium residential and commercial  
- **Foreign access:** Primarily via Premium Residency or approved projects  
- **Price range (apartment, SAR):** 1.5M – 5M+ for quality stock  
- **Why consider:** Mature infrastructure, international schools nearby, corporate proximity  

### King Abdullah Financial District (KAFD)
- **Profile:** Modern towers, mixed-use, business hub  
- **Foreign access:** Commercial units through company entity; residential in specific towers — verify per building  
- **Price range:** Premium — among highest SAR/sqm in city  
- **Why consider:** If your business is finance/services and you want walk-to-work  

### Hittin & adjacent new north
- **Profile:** Villas and compounds, family-oriented  
- **Foreign access:** Limited for individuals; check Premium Residency + specific project approval  
- **Price range (villa, SAR):** 3M – 15M+  
- **Why consider:** Lifestyle, space, expat-friendly compounds (lease more common than buy)  

### ROSHN Communities (e.g., Sedra, Warefa)
- **Profile:** Master-planned, new build, family infrastructure  
- **Foreign access:** **Explicitly marketed to international buyers** in many phases — verify current REGA registration  
- **Price range (villa/townhouse, SAR):** 1.2M – 4M+  
- **Why consider:** Turnkey new build, foreigner-friendly sales process, payment plans  

### Diriyah (Diriyah Gate)
- **Profile:** Heritage luxury, ultra-premium  
- **Foreign access:** Project-specific foreign ownership programs — high-net-worth focus  
- **Price range:** Ultra-luxury — SAR 5M – 50M+  
- **Why consider:** Trophy asset, cultural landmark address, not yield play  

### South / East Riyadh
- **Profile:** More affordable, local buyer dominated  
- **Foreign access:** Generally not targeted at international buyers  
- **Why skip (for most foreigners):** Limited foreign ownership pathways, less international infrastructure

## CHAPTER 4: Foreign Buyer Workflow in Riyadh (9:00–11:00)

**[On camera]**

"**Your step-by-step as a foreign buyer:**

1. **Confirm status** — Premium Residency, approved project, or company purchase?  
2. **Shortlist districts** matching your goal (live / invest / business)  
3. **Engage licensed broker or developer directly** — verify REGA broker license  
4. **Due diligence** — title search on REGA, escrow for off-plan, developer track record  
5. **Reserve & contract** — review with Saudi property lawyer (Arabic contract translation)  
6. **Payment & registration** — REGA title transfer  
7. **Municipality & utilities** — finalize connections  

**Off-plan tip:** ROSHN and similar developers offer staged payment plans. Model total cost including service charges."

## CHAPTER 5: Riyadh vs. Other Options (11:00–12:30)

**[On camera]**

"**Riyadh vs. Jeddah:** Riyadh = capital, government, corporate HQs. Jeddah = Red Sea lifestyle, port, more tourism. Foreign buyers choosing 'Saudi' often pick one based on lifestyle vs. business.

**Riyadh vs. NEOM:** NEOM is 10+ year horizon, off-plan, higher risk/reward. Riyadh is liveable today.

**Riyadh vs. Dubai:** Dubai has mature foreign ownership everywhere, established resale market, easier mortgages. Riyadh offers earlier-cycle pricing and Vision 2030 upside — with more regulatory homework."

## CHAPTER 6: Red Flags in Riyadh Market (12:30–13:30)

1. Unlicensed broker promising 'any nationality, any district'  
2. Off-plan without verified escrow account  
3. Arabic contract you haven't had translated and reviewed  
4. Developer without completed project track record  
5. Price based on 'Dubai will happen here' hype, not current rental demand  
6. Skipping REGA title verification  
7. Buying remote without visiting — fly to Riyadh, walk the district"
