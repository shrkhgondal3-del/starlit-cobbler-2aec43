"With your MISA license in hand, you register your company with the Ministry of Commerce to get your Commercial Registration (CR) , think of it as your company's birth certificate.

Steps:

one. Reserve company name (Arabic and English)

two. Draft and notarize Articles of Association

three. Submit CR application via the Ministry of Commerce portal

four. Pay registration fees

five. Receive CR certificate with your unified national number

Timeline: one–two weeks after MISA approval, assuming documents are clean.

Your CR shows: company name, activities, capital, managers, and registration number. You'll need it for everything , bank account, visas, contracts, tax registration."
