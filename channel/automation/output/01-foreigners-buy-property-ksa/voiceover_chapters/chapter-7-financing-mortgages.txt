"Can foreigners get a mortgage in Saudi Arabia? In limited cases, yes , but it's harder than in Dubai or Europe.

Saudi banks have historically prioritized lending to citizens and residents with stable local income. Premium Residency holders and those with established Saudi income streams have better options.

Most foreign buyers today purchase cash or via international financing secured against assets outside KSA. Don't assume local mortgage pre-approval until you've spoken to a Saudi bank with your specific residency status."
