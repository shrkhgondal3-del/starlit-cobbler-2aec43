"If you're setting up a business in Saudi Arabia anyway , and we'll cover that in our business setup series , your foreign-invested company may acquire commercial property in connection with its licensed activities.

This is common for offices, warehouses, and commercial units. Residential property through a company is a different analysis , often restricted or impractical for individuals.

The path: MISA investment license → Commercial Registration (CR) → property purchase in the company name → register with REGA.
