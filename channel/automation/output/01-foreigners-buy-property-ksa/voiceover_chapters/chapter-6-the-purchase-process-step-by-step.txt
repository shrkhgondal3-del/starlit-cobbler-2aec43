"Here's the typical flow for a qualifying foreign buyer:

Step one: Confirm eligibility for the specific property and zone

Step two: Reserve unit and sign sale agreement

Step three: Deposit to escrow (off-plan) or transfer funds (ready property)

Step four: Obtain any required approvals (MISA, municipality, developer NOC)

Step five: Register title with REGA (Real Estate General Authority)

Step six: Register with municipality and utilities

You'll need: valid passport, proof of funds, eligibility documentation (Premium Residency or foreign ownership approval), and potentially a local representative or lawyer.

Costs to budget:

Property price (obviously)

Registration and transfer fees (typically a percentage of value , verify current REGA fee schedule)

Legal fees

Agent commission (if applicable)

VAT on commercial property (residential may be exempt , confirm with ZATCA guidance)
