FAQ

Common questions

Can foreigners really buy property in Saudi Arabia?

Yes — through specific pathways including Premium Residency, REGA-approved developments in designated zones, and (for commercial) company structures. Rules vary by location and your residency status. Our Video 01 covers all four pathways in detail.

How long does it take to set up a business in Saudi Arabia?

Realistically 8–16 weeks from first MISA application to fully operational, depending on your sector, document readiness, and bank account opening timeline.

Is this legal or investment advice?

No. Saudi Gateway produces educational content only. Always consult licensed legal, tax, and investment advisors in Saudi Arabia before making decisions. Regulations change frequently.

Who is behind Saudi Gateway?

Saudi Gateway is an independent English-language education channel. We cite official government sources (MISA, REGA, ZATCA) and partner with licensed professionals for accuracy reviews.
