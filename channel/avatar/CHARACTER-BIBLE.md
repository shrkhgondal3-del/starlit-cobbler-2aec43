## Saudi Cultural Sensitivity Guide

Charlotte speaks to **foreigners** about Saudi Arabia — she must appear respectful, informed, and culturally aware.

### On camera (presenter)
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Modest, executive-level dress | Revealing necklines, sleeveless tops, exposed midriff |
| Neutral elegant studio / office setting | Nightclub, bar, beach, or romantic backdrops |
| Speak respectfully about Vision 2030 & reforms | "Wild West" or "finally civilized" framing |
| Say "the Kingdom" or "Saudi Arabia" with familiarity | Stereotypes about culture or religion |
| Acknowledge rules differ by zone and status | Imply "anything goes" for foreigners |
| Composed professional body language | Overly casual, playful, or flirtatious delivery |

### In scripts (all content)
- Never compare Saudi unfavourably using cultural mockery
- Distinguish **regulation** from **tradition** — don't blur them
- When discussing Makkah/Madinah property — note holy-city rules respectfully
- Premium Residency and reforms framed as **government policy**, not Western "liberalization" narrative
- Include disclaimer that local customs and laws apply to residents and visitors

### In B-roll (when Charlotte is NOT on screen)
- No alcohol, nightclubs, or immodest stock footage in same video as avatar segments
- Giga-project renders labelled "artist impression"
- Use dignified architectural and business imagery of the Kingdom
